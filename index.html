Our story

A family tradition, grown in Missouri

For us, this business is about more than making products. It's about carrying on a family tradition and a love for lavender that started with Grandma Jean. We love that a little piece of her story is becoming something we can share with others.

Robin has been at the heart of the farm, learning the ins and outs of growing lavender and pouring her heart into developing every product we make. Lavender Silk is one of our first, and after a year of using it ourselves, it's a favorite for its hydration, its silky texture, and its calming aroma.

Thank you for being part of the beginning of Garden Gate Lavender Farm. Your support and word of mouth mean more to us than you know.
